Privacy notice

MyMind is built for caregivers of children who may find it hard to express preferences. We collect as little as possible.

What we store

  • A username and a securely hashed password (no email address).
  • A nickname/avatar for the child (no real name needed), the caregiver questionnaire, consent records and settings.
  • The child's reactions inside the app (likes, favourites, skips) and notes the caregiver records.
  • Subscription and payment-verification records (a reference you provide — never bank passwords or PINs).

Password recovery

If you forget your password you can answer a few questions about the questionnaire you filled in. Answers are compared on our server and are never shown or logged.

What we never do

  • No ads, no public profiles, no chat, no comments, no social tracking.
  • Age check for caretakers. The person who creates an account must be 18 or older. They confirm this once with an official ID through our identity partner, Sumsub, which receives their name, email, date of birth and ID photos for that purpose. MyMind keeps only the result (verified or not, when, and the document's country and type), not the ID, name, email or date of birth.
  • Optional parent links: a caregiver can let a family member see the child's world read-only. Each link is private and unguessable, needs its own view password, can expire, and can be revoked at any time. It shows the child's choices in the app and is never public or searchable. Only create one if you are comfortable sharing that information.
  • No diagnoses: interests are observations of choices, not conclusions about a child's mind or abilities.
  • The child's name, notes or personal details are never sent to YouTube, Spotify or Pexels. Only generic topic words (like “puppy”) are used to search.

Your controls

  • Edit or reset the interest profile any time; delete activity history in My Journey.
  • Download all your data or request account deletion in Settings.

Third parties

Videos play in YouTube's embedded player and music in Spotify's embed; those providers apply their own policies when you press play. Photos come from Pexels with photographer credit.
